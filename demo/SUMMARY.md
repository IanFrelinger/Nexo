**TechCorp International** receives 200+ support tickets daily in multiple languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian). Their current process is:
- ❌ Manual triage takes 2-3 hours daily
- ❌ Language barriers cause delays
- ❌ Sensitive data exposure risks
- ❌ No consistent priority classification
- ❌ Compliance violations go undetected
- ❌ Cost: $500-750 daily ($130,000-195,000 annually)

## ✅ **Nexo's Solution**

Nexo automatically processes support tickets with:
- ✅ **Automated triage** in 15 seconds per ticket
- ✅ **Real-time translation** for 12+ languages
- ✅ **Policy enforcement** for sensitive data
- ✅ **Complete audit trails** for compliance
- ✅ **Works offline** for sensitive environments
- ✅ **Cost: $25 daily** ($6,500 annually)
