# Google Play — Internal testing track (external packaging)

Internal testing on Google Play requires a signed **AAB** produced by your **Android application project**, which is **not** maintained in this repo.

Use **`Ashlar.Client`** from NuGet or REST against **`Ashlar.API`** from your mobile shell. Version codes, `applicationId`, and Play Console steps are owned by that repo’s Android pipeline.

Historical notes in git may reference removed MAUI workflows; ignore them.
