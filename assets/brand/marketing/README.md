## Experimental / Archive

The following AI-generated images are **not used** on the landing page and are kept here for reference only:

- `ashlar-og-commercial-1280x720.png` — AI-generated OG card (collage style, not used)

## Brand Guidelines

All active marketing assets follow brand guidelines from `../BRAND.md`:
- Palette: ink `#2B2420`, cream `#F7F2E5`, oat `#EFE8D8`, sage `#7E8F6E`, gold `#D1A23C`, clay `#C96F4A`, olive `#4A5540`
- Official mark: node-"o" (bullseye + gold scribble ring)
- Gold = certified (used sparingly, never decorative spam)
- Voice: precise, technical, premium craft
- Aesthetic: clean, typographic, modern developer-tool (not AI collage/poster)

## GitHub Social Preview Options

Repository Settings → General → Social preview should use:
- `../ashlar-social-card-1280x640.png` (preferred, existing clean design)

## Future Assets

When creating new marketing assets:
- **Do:** Clean typography, high contrast, official brand SVGs, minimal flat design
- **Don't:** Collage, masking tape, handwritten doodles, gold seals, sparkles, faux 3D, cinematic lighting, AI illustration aesthetics
